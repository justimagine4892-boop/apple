About Hufsa

A therapist who goes beyond the hour.

I've spent my career working with people many clinicians turn away: those living with serious mental illness, addiction, and complex trauma. I keep a deliberately small caseload so I can do the work that happens between sessions — coordinating with psychiatrists and treatment teams, checking in during hard weeks, and continuing to study the latest research so your care stays current.

  • Licensed in California · in person in Irvine and via telehealth statewide
  • Care coordination with psychiatrists, hospitals and programs
  • Culturally attuned care, including for Muslim and South Asian clients

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you take insurance?

I'm out-of-network. I provide a superbill each month that you submit to your PPO plan; many clients are reimbursed 50–80% after their deductible. I'm happy to help you check your benefits before we start.

Who is a good fit for therapy with you?

Adults in California living with complex PTSD, bipolar disorder, psychosis, or addiction — especially people who've felt misunderstood by past care or who need a therapist who coordinates with their treatment team.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. I see clients in person in Irvine and by secure video anywhere in California.

Do you work with psychiatrists?

Yes. With your permission I communicate regularly with your psychiatrist and other providers so your care is connected.

Is there a lower-cost option?

I hold a small number of reduced-fee spots, including for college students. Ask about availability during your consultation.
